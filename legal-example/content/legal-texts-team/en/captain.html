PROVISIONS

ON THE TEAM CAPTAIN IN THE ACE BATTLE RUN SPORTS ECOSYSTEM

ACE BATTLE RUN

§ 1. General Provisions

  1. These Provisions define the status, rules for appointment, scope of functions, powers, duties and principles of liability of the Team Captain (hereinafter: the “Captain”) operating within the ACE BATTLE RUN ecosystem.

  2. ACE BATTLE RUN is an ecosystem for the organization and provision of services related to individual and team & individual sports competitions conducted by authorized or licensed operators within a specified territory, in accordance with the relevant competition regulations and the rules of the ACE BATTLE ASSOCIATION (ABA).

  3. The Captain is a person designated to act as the leader and representative of the Team within competitions, as well as other sporting events organized within the ACE BATTLE RUN ecosystem.

  4. The Captain may perform an initiating role in the formation of a Team, organization of its composition, and coordination of the participation of athletes (RUNNERS) in competitions.

  5. The Captain acts within the scope of powers defined by these Provisions, the Regulations of a specific competition, as well as within the scope of powers granted by the Organizer, Operator, Club, Coach or Manager — if such powers have been granted.

  6. The Captain is not an employee, attorney-in-fact or statutory representative of the Organizer, Operator, ABA or ACE BATTLE RUN, unless otherwise provided by a separate document or legal relationship.

  7. The mere performance of the function of Captain does not give rise to personal liability on the part of the Captain for the obligations of the Organizer, Operator, Club, ABA or other participants in competitions.

  8. Where a Manager or Coach is present within the Team, the Captain cooperates with such persons in accordance with the division of responsibilities applicable within the relevant Team.

  9. In the absence of a Manager or Coach, the Captain may contact the Organizer or Operator of the competition directly in matters concerning their Team.

  10. The activities of the Captain are governed by:

  1. these Provisions;

  2. the regulations and rules of specific competitions;

  3. the applicable rules of the ACE BATTLE ASSOCIATION (ABA);

  4. the regulations of the Club or Team — where applicable;

  5. mandatory provisions of law.

§ 2. Objectives and Tasks of the Captain

  1. The primary objectives of the Captain’s activities include:

  1. ensuring the proper organization of the Team’s participation in competitions;

  2. coordinating the activities of Team members;

  3. supporting proper communication between athletes and the Organizer;

  4. supporting compliance with the principles of sports competition, fair play and sports ethics;

  5. supporting integration and the building of a positive atmosphere within the Team;

  6. representing the Team within the scope of powers granted to the Captain;

  7. supporting the development of the ACE BATTLE RUN ecosystem at the Team, Club and regional levels.

  1. The Captain should perform their functions with respect for the dignity, safety, privacy and rights of all participants in competitions.

§ 3. Functions of the Captain

3.1. Organizational and Coordination Functions

  1. The Captain coordinates the participation of members of their Team in competitions.

  2. In particular, the Captain may:

  1. provide athletes with information concerning the organization of competitions;

  2. coordinate the gathering of the Team before the start;

  3. organize athletes’ preparation for participation in competitions;

  4. coordinate athletes’ completion of pre-start procedures;

  5. provide information regarding the order, place or time of the start;

  6. cooperate with the Coach, Manager and Strategist, if such persons have been designated within the Team;

  7. in the absence of a Coach or Manager, perform organizational activities necessary for the proper participation of the Team, to the extent permitted by the Regulations of the competition.

  1. The Captain is not authorized to issue instructions contrary to the Regulations of the competition, the instructions of judges, safety rules or applicable law.

3.2. Administrative Functions

  1. The Captain may cooperate with the Organizer or Operator with regard to:

  1. providing information concerning the composition of the Team;

  2. organizing the collection of race numbers, chips, equipment among Team members or other equipment items;

  3. providing athletes with organizational information;

  4. monitoring athlete registration deadlines;

  5. reporting identified errors concerning data or registrations to the Organizer.

  1. The Captain may use the ACE BATTLE RUN platform exclusively within the scope of powers granted by the system Administrator or Organizer.

  2. The Captain is not authorized to obtain access to the personal data of Team members that are not necessary for the performance of their functions.

  3. The Captain is obliged to maintain the confidentiality of information and personal data to which they have gained access in connection with the performance of their functions.

  4. The Captain is not authorized to disclose the personal data of Team members to third parties without an appropriate legal basis or authorization having been granted.

3.3. Representative Functions

  1. The Captain may represent the Team in contacts with:

  1. the Organizer;

  2. the Operator of the competition;

  3. judges;

  4. representatives of the ACE BATTLE ASSOCIATION;

  5. representatives of other Teams;

  6. other persons or entities connected with the organization of competitions — to the extent concerning the participation of the Team.

  1. The Captain participates in meetings and seminars, opening and closing ceremonies of competitions, media events and Team meetings.

  2. The Captain may submit organizational statements, protests or appeals on behalf of the Team only to the extent permitted by the Regulations of the specific competition.

  3. The Captain is not authorized to assume financial or legal obligations on behalf of the Organizer, Operator, ABA, Club or other entities unless they have separate authorization to do so.

  4. The signing by the Captain of a document concerning the participation of the Team does not automatically constitute the assumption by the Captain of a personal financial or legal obligation.

3.4. Motivational and Educational Functions

  1. The Captain supports the development of an atmosphere of cooperation, mutual respect and sports competition.

  2. In particular, their tasks include:

  1. motivating Team members;

  2. supporting new athletes;

  3. promoting the principles of fair play;

  4. preventing conduct that violates the dignity of other persons;

  5. providing athletes with information about the principles, objectives and values of the ACE BATTLE ASSOCIATION;

  6. supporting the positive image of the Team and the ACE BATTLE RUN ecosystem.

  1. The Captain is not authorized to use violence, threats, discrimination, humiliation, harassment or other forms of impermissible conduct towards athletes.

§ 4. Rights of the Captain

  1. The Captain has the right, within the limits arising from these Provisions and the Regulations of the specific competition, to:

  1. represent the Team before the Organizer and Operator;

  2. obtain information necessary for the proper organization of the Team’s participation;

  3. make decisions concerning the Team’s composition and participation tactics in the absence of a Strategist or Coach;

  4. require participants to comply with rules, discipline and standards of conduct;

  5. provide the Organizer with comments concerning the organization of competitions;

  6. report identified violations;

  7. submit protests and appeals in the cases and within the time limits specified in the Regulations of the competition;

  8. participate in briefings, organizational meetings and other events related to competitions;

  9. cooperate with the Coach, Manager, Strategist and other Team officials.

  1. The Captain may request the Organizer to provide information concerning the organization of competitions if such information is necessary for the performance of their functions.

  2. The powers of the Captain may not infringe upon the exclusive competence of the Organizer, Operator, judges or other persons authorized under the Regulations of the competition.

§ 5. Duties of the Captain

  1. The Captain is obliged to:

  1. comply with these Provisions;

  2. comply with the Regulations of the competition;

  3. respect the decisions of judges and persons responsible for safety;

  4. provide Team members with important organizational information;

  5. support compliance with the principles of fair play;

  6. immediately inform the Organizer of significant organizational problems concerning the Team;

  7. protect confidential information and personal data to which they have gained access;

  8. act in a manner that does not harm the good name of the Team, the Organizer or the ACE BATTLE RUN ecosystem.

  1. The Captain is not authorized to knowingly provide the Organizer or Operator with information which they know to be untrue.

  2. If the Captain is uncertain as to the correctness of an athlete’s data, they should contact the athlete or the Organizer for the purpose of verification.

§ 6. Liability of the Captain

  1. The Captain is liable for their own actions and omissions related to the performance of their functions, in accordance with applicable law and these Provisions.

  2. The mere fact of performing the function of Captain does not give rise to their personal liability for the actions or omissions of other Team members.

  3. The Captain may be liable for the actions of a Team member exclusively within the limits arising from:

  1. their own culpable action or omission;

  2. a separate legal relationship;

  3. the powers granted to them;

  4. mandatory provisions of law.

  1. In particular, the Captain is not personally liable for:

  1. a violation of the Regulations by an athlete, provided that the Captain themselves did not commit such violation and did not contribute to it through their action or omission;

  2. the athlete’s state of health;

  3. the athlete’s individual decisions concerning participation in competitions;

  4. the athlete’s personal belongings;

  5. the athlete’s financial obligations;

  6. damage caused by an athlete to third parties, unless the Captain’s liability arises from provisions of law or from their own culpable action.

  1. The Captain should, within the limits of their actual ability, respond to conduct by Team members that may create a safety risk or violate the Regulations of the competition, in particular by informing the relevant Organizer, judge or person responsible for safety.

§ 7. Personal Data and Confidentiality

  1. The Captain may obtain access to Team members’ data exclusively to the extent necessary for the performance of their functions.

  2. Personal data should be processed in accordance with applicable law, in particular in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR).

  3. The Captain is obliged to:

  1. use data exclusively for purposes related to organizing the Team’s participation;

  2. ensure data confidentiality;

  3. not disclose data to unauthorized persons;

  4. apply appropriate data protection measures;

  5. immediately inform the Organizer or system Administrator of any suspected loss, disclosure or unauthorized access to data.

  1. The Captain is not authorized to independently publish athletes’ personal data on the Internet, social media or other publicly accessible locations unless they have an appropriate legal basis or authorization.

§ 8. Protests and Appeals

  1. The Captain may submit a protest or appeal in matters concerning the Team if the Regulations of the specific competition provide for such possibility.

  2. Unless the Regulations of the competition provide otherwise, an appeal against a judge’s decision may be submitted within 24 hours from the end of the relevant race or competition event.

  3. An appeal shall be submitted electronically to the official e-mail address indicated by the Organizer or through the system indicated in the Regulations of the competition.

  4. If a person performing the function of Coach or Manager has been designated within the Team, the appeal shall be submitted by the person authorized in accordance with the internal division of responsibilities or the Regulations of the competition.

  5. In the absence of a Coach or Manager, the appeal may be submitted by the Captain.

  6. An appeal should contain at least:

  1. the name of the Team;

  2. the details of the person submitting the appeal;

  3. a description of the decision to which it relates;

  4. the grounds;

  5. the request;

  6. information enabling identification of the race or competition event.

  1. Appeals submitted after the expiry of the deadline may be left without consideration if the relevant Regulations of the competition do not provide for the possibility of restoring the deadline.

  2. The submission of an appeal does not suspend the execution of the judge’s decision unless the Regulations of the competition provide otherwise.

§ 9. Appointment of the Captain

  1. The Captain may be appointed by:

  1. the Organizer or Operator — in the case of the initial formation of a Team;

  2. the Club;

  3. the Team Manager;

  4. the Coach;

  5. Team members — if provided for by the regulations of the Club or Team.

  1. The appointment of the Captain should be registered in the ACE BATTLE RUN ecosystem or in another form adopted by the Organizer.

  2. The Organizer may establish additional requirements concerning the performance of the function of Captain if justified by organizational considerations or competition safety.

§ 10. Suspension or Revocation of the Captain’s Powers

  1. The Captain’s powers may be suspended or revoked in the event of:

  1. a violation of these Provisions;

  2. a serious or repeated violation of the Regulations of the competition;

  3. a violation of fair play principles;

  4. a violation of personal data protection principles;

  5. actions creating a safety risk to participants;

  6. a gross violation of the principles of cooperation with the Organizer or judges;

  7. loss of the conditions necessary to perform the function.

  1. A decision to suspend or revoke the Captain’s powers may be made by the entity that appointed the Captain, as well as by the Organizer or Operator — to the extent concerning participation in competitions organized by that entity, if justified by the regulations or competition safety requirements.

  2. Before a decision to suspend or revoke the Captain’s powers is made, the Captain should, where possible, be given an opportunity to provide explanations.

  3. In cases requiring immediate action for safety reasons, the Organizer may temporarily suspend the Captain’s powers with immediate effect.

§ 11. Relations with the Organizer, Operator and ABA

  1. The Captain performs their functions within the ACE BATTLE RUN ecosystem and is obliged to respect the competences of the Organizer and Operator of the specific competition.

  2. The competence of judges regarding the assessment of the conduct of competitions, results and violations of the Regulations takes precedence over the Captain’s organizational instructions.

  3. The Captain is not authorized to interfere with the independence of judges’ decisions.

  4. The Captain may raise objections and submit protests exclusively in accordance with the procedure provided for in the Regulations of the competition.

  5. The ACE BATTLE ASSOCIATION may establish additional rules concerning the functioning of Captains within the ACE BATTLE RUN ecosystem.

§ 12. Safety Rules

  1. The safety of participants takes precedence over the achievement of sporting or organizational objectives.

  2. The Captain should immediately provide the Organizer or the relevant services with information about a situation that may pose a threat to the life or health of participants.

  3. The Captain is not the person responsible for the medical provision of the competition unless they have been separately designated for this purpose and possess the appropriate authorizations.

  4. Decisions concerning the provision of medical assistance, interruption of competitions or withdrawal of an athlete shall be made by persons authorized in accordance with the Regulations of the competition and applicable law.

§ 13. Image and Contact with the Mass Media

  1. The Captain may participate in media events, conferences, opening and closing ceremonies, as well as other events aimed at promoting ACE BATTLE RUN.

  2. Publication of an individually identifiable image of Team members requires an appropriate legal basis.

  3. The Captain is not authorized to make statements on behalf of the Organizer, Operator or ABA producing legal effects unless expressly authorized to do so.

  4. Statements made by the Captain as a representative of the Team should comply with these Provisions and the principles of sports ethics.

§ 14. No Remuneration and Nature of the Function

  1. The performance of the function of Captain is organizational and sporting in nature and, in itself, does not create an employment relationship, contract of mandate, contract for specific work or any other legal relationship with the Organizer, Operator, ABA or ACE BATTLE RUN.

  2. Any remuneration, reimbursement of expenses or other benefits related to the performance of the function of Captain require a separate agreement.

§ 15. Final Provisions

  1. These Provisions constitute the basic document defining the principles governing the functioning of the Captain within the ACE BATTLE RUN sports ecosystem.

  2. In matters not regulated by these Provisions, the following shall apply:

  1. the Regulations of the specific competition;

  2. the rules of the ACE BATTLE ASSOCIATION;

  3. the relevant regulations of the Club or Team;

  4. applicable provisions of law.

  1. In the event of any conflict between these Provisions and mandatory provisions of law, the provisions of law shall prevail.

  2. In the event of any conflict between these Provisions and the Regulations of a specific competition with respect to the organization and conduct of that specific competition, the Regulations of that competition shall prevail.

  3. The Organizer or the relevant entity shall have the right to update these Provisions in the event of organizational changes, changes within the ACE BATTLE RUN ecosystem or changes in provisions of law.

  4. The current version of the Provisions shall be made available in a manner enabling the Captain to familiarize themselves with its contents before commencing the performance of the function of Captain.

  5. These Provisions shall enter into force on the date of their adoption.
