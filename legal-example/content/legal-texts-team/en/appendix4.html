ACE BATTLE RUN (TEAM MILE POLAND)

FOR DETERMINING THE TEAM LEVEL

To § 8. TEAM FORMAT AND RANKING

This Appendix establishes the time-based criteria for the individual ranking, applied to determine the appropriate INDIVIDUAL LEVEL of team participants in the ACE BATTLE RUN RATING system.

The individual level is determined on the basis of the participant’s official result over a distance of one mile (1,609.344 m), in accordance with the thresholds specified below.

2. RULES FOR APPLYING THE TABLE

  1. The INDIVIDUAL LEVEL is a numerical indicator of the participant’s level in the ACE BATTLE RUN RATING system.

  2. For the purposes of this Appendix, the following participant categories shall apply:

  • RUNNER — a participant competing in the corresponding RUNNER role;

  • ACE — a participant competing in the ACE role;

  • JOKER — a participant competing in the JOKER role.

  1. Depending on the participant’s gender and role in the team race, the relevant column of the table shall apply.

  2. For the men’s category, the following criteria shall apply:

  • Men — RUNNERS;

  • Men — ACE & JOKER.

  1. For the women’s category, the following criteria shall apply:

  • Women — RUNNERS;

  • Women — ACE & JOKER.

  1. For a mixed team, the following criteria shall apply:

  • Mixed Team — RUNNERS;

  • Mixed Team — ACE & JOKER.

  1. The participant’s official result obtained shall be used to determine the participant’s individual level in accordance with this table.

  2. The participant’s individual level may be used to determine the team composition, team ranking, division of teams into levels and divisions, as well as eligibility for subsequent competitions within the ACE BATTLE RUN system, if provided for by the applicable rules.

  3. The rules for determining the final individual ranking, including the rules for taking into account the participant’s best result, shall apply in accordance with § 13 of the Regulations and the applicable ACE BATTLE RUN RATING rules.

  4. In the event that one participant has several official results, the rules for determining the best official result specified in the main Regulations and the applicable rules of the ranking system shall apply.

  5. In the event of a change to the ACE BATTLE RUN RATING rules, this table may be updated in accordance with the procedure specified in the Regulations.

3. USE OF INDIVIDUAL LEVELS IN THE TEAM RANKING

  1. The team ranking race is intended to determine the team’s result and its ranking level.

  2. Each participant shall receive an individual result and a position in the relevant classification.

  3. The participant’s individual result may constitute the basis for granting the participant TEAM RUNNER status in the ACE BATTLE RUN RATING system.

  4. The individual levels of participants shall be used, in accordance with the established rules, to create and divide teams into the appropriate levels and divisions.

  5. The individual classification shall be maintained separately for men and women.

  6. The team classification shall be maintained according to the following categories:

  7. men’s teams;

  8. women’s teams;

  9. mixed teams.

  10. Specific requirements concerning team composition, compliance with participants’ levels, and eligibility conditions for subsequent leagues or competitions may be specified in separate ACE BATTLE RUN RATING documents.

4. STATUS OF THE APPENDIX

  1. This Appendix No. 4 constitutes an integral part of the Public Regulations of the national ranking competitions ACE BATTLE RUN – TEAM MILE POLAND.

  2. This Appendix shall apply in conjunction with § 8 “Team Format and Ranking” and § 13 “Individual Ranking” of the main Regulations.

  3. In the event of any conflict between this Appendix and mandatory provisions of law, the provisions of law shall prevail.

  4. In the event of any conflict between this Appendix and the main Regulations with respect to provisions governing a specific competition, the provisions of the main Regulations shall prevail, unless the applicable ACE BATTLE RUN RATING rules expressly provide otherwise.

  5. The current version of this Appendix shall be published by the Organizer on the official information and registration resource poland.acebattle.run.
