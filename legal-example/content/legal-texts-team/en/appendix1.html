EVENT SCHEDULE FOR PARTICIPANTS ON THE DAY OF THE START

Team-Individual Ranking Mile

ACE BATTLE RUN (TEAM MILE POLAND)

This section constitutes an appendix to the Regulations of the sports event ACE BATTLE RUN (TEAM MILE) TEAM-Individual Ranking (Poland) and the Public Provisions applicable to the “Team-Individual Ranking Mile (Team Mile Poland)” format. In matters not regulated by this Appendix, the general provisions of the Regulations and Public Provisions shall apply, including those concerning personal data protection, participants’ image, safety and liability.

Below is the schedule for the day of the start, addressed to the Participants of the Event. This document is of a general and informational nature and does not replace the Event Regulations, the Public Provisions or other official documents. The current order of events is announced on site by the announcer and the panel of judges.

WHAT TAKES PLACE AND WHEN

Morning Race

Time What happens What the participant needs to know
9:00 Opening of registration and Check-in for race participants. Start of admission to the venue
9:00–9:45 Present an identity document, collect your race number and chip; confirm your role in the race. Registration window — do not leave it until the last minute
9:45 Closing of registration and Check-in. No new entries are accepted; without Check-in — DNS status
9:45–9:59 Team assembly in the Call Room; collection of role attributes (MACE — for Jokers, chip on the left leg, wristband colour — Runner role); taking positions at the start line, introduction of team participants. Be on site — the announcer will call you by your name and role
9:59:00–9:59:30 Participants proceed to the pre-start line upon the Starter’s instruction. Final readiness check by the Judge
9:59:30–9:59:50

Command “on your marks, attention” — gun signal.

 Start of the first race; subsequent races — every 15 minutes according to the schedule
After each race The preliminary result is published no later than 2 minutes after the actual finish. Protest may be submitted within 30 minutes from the time of publication
After the last race Final, undisputed results are published. Results with the status “Under Review” are published after the decision of the panel of judges
In the event of a programme delay The times of all subsequent starts, deadlines for submitting protests and publication times are shifted proportionally. Follow the actual announcement times at the venue, not the schedule
~30 minutes after the last race Publication of all Official Results on the website. The venue may be left

Important: arrive early and complete Check-in

in the morning between 9:00–9:45 (morning race)

in the evening between 17:00–17:45 (evening race)

— in the morning after 9:45, and in the evening after 17:45, registration is closed and no new entries are accepted.

Carefully follow announcements concerning your race number, name and role in the team.
