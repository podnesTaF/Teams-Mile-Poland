2. Purposes of personal data processing

The participant's personal data may be processed for the following purposes:

a) organization and conduct of competitions

In particular:

  • registration of the participant;

  • confirmation of his identity;

  • organization of the start;

  • assignment to the correct category;

  • contact with the participant in organizational matters;

  • ensuring the proper course of the competition.

b) measuring time and determining the result

In particular:

  • recording of running time;

  • determining the location;

  • preparation of classification;

  • verification of the result;

  • Handling protests and complaints.

c) making official results publicly available,

In order to:

  • documenting the results of the competition;

  • ensure the transparency of sport classification;

  • allow participants to check their own score;

  • allow the results to be verified by other participants and interested parties;

  • documenting the history of organized sports events.

d) security

to the extent necessary to:

  • ensuring the safety of participants;

  • taking action in emergency situations;

  • contact with the person indicated by the participant as an emergency contact person.

e) establishing, pursuing or defending claims

in the event of claims related to the organization or course of the event.

f) performance of legal obligations

if the obligation to process data results from the provisions of law.

g) payment of prizes and tax settlements related to prizes

if the participant takes the place awarded in accordance with the Regulations, in order to identify the winner, accounting and tax documentation of the payment of the cash prize and - to the extent that the value of the prize exceeds the exemption threshold specified in Article 21(1)(68) of the Personal Income Tax Act of 26 July 1991 - the collection of flat-rate income tax referred to in Article 30(1)(2) of that Act.

3. Legal bases for processing

Depending on the purpose, the Administrator may process personal data on the basis of:

3.1. Art. 6 ust. 1 lit. b RODO

– if the processing is necessary for the performance of the contract or to take actions at the request of the person prior to the conclusion of the agreement, to the extent related to participation in the event.

3.2. Art. 6 ust. 1 lit. c RODO

– if the processing is necessary for the performance of a legal obligation incumbent on the Administrator.

3.3. Art. 6 ust. 1 lit. f RODO

– if the processing is necessary to pursue a legitimate interest of the Controller or a third party, unless the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject are of an overriding nature.

This applies in particular to the public disclosure of official competition results to the extent specified in the Regulations.

Prior to the publication of the results, the Controller has conducted or keeps a Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) test on the application of Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.

3.4. Art. 6 ust. 1 lit. a RODO

– only in cases where the processing is genuinely based on the participant's voluntary consent, in particular with regard to specific image-related activities.

4. Public publication of the results of the competition

The Administrator informs that the official results of the competition can be published publicly on the Internet, without the need for registration or login of the user.

The public leaderboard may include:

  • name;

  • surname;

  • running time;

  • place/position in the ranking;

  • sport category, if applicable;

  • the score or number of points, if they are part of the classification.

The administrator does not publish in the scoreboard:

  • PESEL number;

  • date of birth;

  • address of residence;

  • Your phone number

  • Email address

  • the details of the emergency contact person;

  • health information;

  • other contact or organizational data.

The public publication of the results takes place to the extent specified in the Event Regulations and within the framework of the Administrator's legitimate interest referred to in Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR, if this condition applies.

Such an approach is in line with the current line of the Personal Data Protection Office concerning sports competitions: the Personal Data Protection Office indicated that the publication of data of competition participants may be admissible, but the scope of data must be adequate to the purpose of publication.

5. Why results are available without registration

The administrator has adopted the principle that the official results of the competition are available to all interested persons without the need to:

  • create an account;

  • login;

  • provide an email address;

  • provide other personal data.

The purpose of such a solution is to ensure the real availability of the official sports classification, in particular for:

  • participants of the competition;

  • other participants;

  • trainers;

  • sports clubs;

  • organizers of subsequent events;

  • Performance verifiers;

  • other people interested in the event.

A detailed justification for this solution can be found in the Administrator's internal Balance Test (LIA).

6. Scope of data collected during registration

Depending on the organizational needs, the Administrator may process:

  • name;

  • surname;

  • date of birth;

  • phone number;

  • Email address

  • address;

  • details of the person to contact in an emergency;

  • data on participation and sports result.

  • bank account number and data necessary for tax settlement - only if the participant wins a cash prize.

The scope of data may vary depending on the registration method and organizational requirements.

The data collected for registration purposes is not automatically intended for publication.

7. Health data

If the participant voluntarily provides the Administrator with information concerning his/her health, such data may constitute a special category of personal data within the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR.

The Administrator does not publish health data in the results of the competition.

Health data may only be processed to the extent and on the basis of legal permission by applicable law.

8. Data recipients

Personal data may be transferred to entities that support the Administrator in the organization of the event, in particular:

  • providers of registration systems;

  • suppliers of timing systems;

  • IT service providers;

  • providers of hosting and maintenance of the website;

  • service providers;

  • entities providing accounting or legal services;

  • entities authorized to obtain data on the basis of legal provisions;

  • medical services or entities – if it is necessary in a situation of danger to health or life.

The data provided to service providers is limited to the extent necessary to carry out the task in question.

9. Public Audience of Results

The data contained in the official leaderboard may be available for:

an unlimited range of recipients using the Internet.

No registration or login is required to access the results.

The Administrator cannot guarantee that third parties will not copy or save information that has been lawfully published to the public.

For this reason, the Administrator limits the scope of data published in the results to data directly related to the sports result.

10. Data retention period

10.1. Organisational data of the participant

The data processed in connection with the organization of the event will be stored for the period necessary to achieve this purpose, and then for the period necessary to:

  • handling complaints;

  • establishing, pursuing or defending claims;

  • compliance with legal obligations.

As a rule, the participant's organizational data will be stored for a period of 5 years from the end of the calendar year in which the competition took place, unless the law or a specific case requires a longer period.

10.2. Official Results

The official results including the name, surname and sports result will be publicly available for a period of 5 years from the date of the competition.

After the expiry of this period, the Administrator:

  • delete publicly available personal data,

or

  • if there is a separate legal basis for further storage or publication, restrict the scope of the data or take other lawful action.

10.3. Claims

If the data is necessary for the establishment, exercise or defence of claims, it may be stored for a period corresponding to the limitation period of the claim in question.

10.4. Legal obligations

If the law imposes an obligation on the Administrator to store certain data for a longer period, the data will be stored for the period resulting from these provisions.

The Participant is entitled, on the terms set out in the GDPR, to:

  1. access to your personal data;

  2. obtain a copy of the data;

  3. rectification of data;

  4. erasure of data – in the cases specified in Article 17 of the GDPR;

  5. restriction of processing;

  6. data portability – where this right applies;

  7. object to data processing;

  8. withdrawal of consent – if the processing is based on consent;

  9. lodge a complaint with the President of the Office for Personal Data Protection.

14. Voluntary provision of data

Providing the data required to register and organize participation in the competition is voluntary, but it may be necessary to be admitted to participate in the competition.

Providing the data necessary to determine the result is necessary to the extent that without this data it is not possible to correctly determine and document the sports result.

15. Automated decision-making

Participants' data is not used to make decisions that produce legal effects against them or similarly significantly affect them only by automated means.

16. Transfer of data outside the EEA
