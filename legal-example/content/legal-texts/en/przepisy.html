2. REGISTRATION AND CONFIRMATION OF PARTICIPATION

2.1. Mandatory registration. Each participant of the qualifying races must register at poland.acebattle.run and register for the event in person. On the day of the competition, applications for participation are accepted as an exception from 09:00 to 09:30, provided that technical capabilities are obtained.

2.2. Confirmation. Participation is considered confirmed after registration on the website and assignment of Confirmed status with a confirmed QR code. The initial application itself does not give acceptance.

2.4. Check-in. From 09:00 to 09:30, the participant presents an identity document, confirms profile data and receives a start number.

2.5. Closing of registration. At 09:30, registration and check-in are closed. New applications are not accepted. A pre-registered participant who has not completed check-in is given DNS status and cannot start work.

2.6. Recruitment. Individuals who are at least 18 years old and have accepted the rules in their personal account on the site are eligible to participate.

Subsequent runs – every 15 minutes according to the schedule, submission 10 minutes earlier, readiness 3 minutes earlier. The number of runs and participants of each run is determined by the Organizing Committee after the end of the Check-in, taking into account the actual number of participants, technical possibilities of timing, safety and the time of use of the facility.

Participants' preference for starting together with family, friends or another group is considered a priority, provided that the group declares that they will cover the mile within the allotted time and their joint start does not pose a safety risk.

If there is no such preference, participants are separated as evenly as possible, according to the declared or previously confirmed time: 6:00-7:00, 7:00-9:00 and 9:00-12:00 min/mile. Mixed and mixed sex runs are allowed.

A joint start does not create a team: each participant receives an individual score, and the rating and prizes are awarded separately for men and women. The final line-ups and the number of qualifying heats are determined on the start list after briefing.

The break between starts is 15 minutes. 10 minutes before the start, participants gather in the phone room, and 3 minutes before it, they must be ready at the entrance to the track.

Entry to the track is allowed only on the referee's order after the distance and finish zone have been fully covered. Participants in the next run are not allowed to cross the track or interfere with the current run.

The control time for running the mile is 12:00. A participant who has not completed the distance within the allotted time must stop the race at the referee's order, safely leave the track and receive a DNF.

The next heat is assembled at the same time as the previous one. A new start is only granted after the track has been cleared, the timing chips have been checked and confirmed by the starter.

6. RACE RULES AND TIMING

The start is given from a standing position from a common curved starting line over a distance of one mile. After takeoff, you are allowed to assume an inside position without pushing, blocking or creating a dangerous situation. Shortening the distance, outside assistance and using someone else's bib number are prohibited. The participant responsible for the false start will be disqualified.

The official countdown of the time of these competitions is carried out using the race results system. Each participant is identified by his or her starting number and receives an individual result and ranking position. Manual time recording, referee end order, and video recording serve as supplies. In the event of discrepancies, the chief judge makes a decision based on all available data: full name, start number, time, place in the male or female category, and sports status.

7. RESULTS, PROTESTS AND EVALUATIONS

  • COMPLETED — The participant has covered the full distance; the result is confirmed.

  • DNS — Didn't start, including no check-in before 09:30.

  • DNF — Didn't finish.

  • DSQ — Disqualified; the protocol sets out the rule and the judge's decision.

The preliminary result of each run is published no later than 10 minutes after its actual completion. The protest is filed within 30 minutes of publication. In the event of a program delay, all related deadlines are calculated based on the actual time of the respective action.

The object of the protest may only be: an error in the identification of the participant or number; an error in the time or order of the finish line as a result of the race; incorrect lap counting; interference, pushing, blocking or shortening of the distance; incorrect DNS, DNF or DSQ status; category error; violation of the start procedure that could affect the result.

Dissatisfaction with the recorded time or place, as well as a request to move the participant higher without indicating a specific error or violation, are not the subject of the protest.

The participant or his representative submits a protest using the feedback form in the participant's personal account, providing the heat number, start number, disputed action or indicator and the required correction. A protest does not stop the program. If the available data is sufficient, the chief judge and the secretary review it during the next run.

If time measurement data, video recordings or other technical materials are required, the disputed result is given the status "Under Review". The undisputed results are published without delay; The disputed score and rating are corrected after a decision has been made. All protests and appeals must be resolved before the final evaluation and awards are approved.

The decision on the protest is communicated to the applicant. The appeal is filed within 15 minutes of receipt of the decision and is considered by a committee consisting of three people who did not participate in the initial decision. The appeal does not stop the program and concerns only the disputed result and the positions dependent on it.

The individual score takes into account the participant's best official result during the qualifying period. In the event of a tie, the second best result is considered, and in the event of a lack of a tie or repeated tie, the previous best time achieved. The result determines the individual level.

The competition is held in two categories: men and women. Age subcategories are not used. The prize fund in the qualification cycle is PLN 5,000 and PLN 2,500 in each category.

8.1. Conditions for the payment of the prize. The condition for the payment of the cash prize is: confirmed status of the "COMPLETED" result of the participant; proper completion of the procedure of protests and appeals regarding a given result; providing the Organizer with the data necessary for the payment and settlement of the prize, including name, surname, bank account number and – only if necessary for tax settlement – PESEL or NIP number. The cash prize is paid by bank transfer within 30 days from the date of determining the final classification, unless the Organizer and the participant agree on a different date or method of payment.

8.2. Tax settlement of prizes. Pursuant to Article 21(1)(68) of the Personal Income Tax Act of 26 July 1991, the value of a cash prize awarded in a competition in the field of sport is exempt from income tax if its one-off value does not exceed PLN 2,000. The amount of prizes specified in these Regulations does not exceed this amount, so the Organizer does not charge an advance or lump-sum income tax from them. If, in a given or subsequent edition of the Event, the Organizer awards a cash prize exceeding the indicated threshold, the Organizer — as the remitter — will collect a lump-sum income tax on such a prize in accordance with Article 30(1)(2) of this Act.

8.3. Failure to claim the prize. Failure by the eligible participant to provide the data necessary for the payment of the prize within 90 days from the date of the prize ceremony shall result in the expiration of the claim for payment of the prize, without prejudice to mandatory provisions of law. The Organizer reserves the right to withhold the payment of the prize for the duration of the investigation into the violation of these Regulations by a given participant.

9. SAFETY AND MEDICAL ASSISTANCE

The medical point is open from 08:30 until the last participant leaves. The facility is equipped with an AED, a first aid kit, communication with 112 emergency services, water and a recovery zone.

The participant is obliged to stop the race at the request of a medical professional. The medical director has the right to withdraw the participant for health reasons; the event director and the main referee have the right to interrupt or postpone the start in the event of a safety threat.
