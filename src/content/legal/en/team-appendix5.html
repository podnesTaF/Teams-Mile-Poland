How Ranking Competitions Are Conducted

Ranking competitions are conducted in accordance with the established ACE BATTLE RUN – TEAM MILE POLAND team ranking criteria, taking into account the applicable division, team level, and the time achieved by the team.

The team result is determined on the basis of the time achieved by the team participants in the relevant category, in accordance with the criteria set out in this Appendix.

STATUS OF THE APPENDIX

  1. This Appendix No. 5 constitutes an integral part of the Public Rules and Regulations of the national ranking competition ACE BATTLE RUN – TEAM MILE POLAND.

  2. This Appendix shall be applied jointly with § 8 “Team Format and Ranking” of the main Rules and Regulations.

  3. In the event of any conflict between this Appendix and mandatory provisions of law, the provisions of law shall prevail.

  4. In the event of any conflict between this Appendix and the main Rules and Regulations with respect to matters governing a specific competition, the provisions of the main Rules and Regulations shall prevail, unless the relevant ACE BATTLE RUN RATING rules expressly provide otherwise.

  5. The current version of this Appendix shall be published by the Organizer on the official information and registration resource poland.acebattle.run.
