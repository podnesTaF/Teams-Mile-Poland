Team–Individual Ranking Mile

ACE BATTLE RUN (TEAM MILE POLAND)

This section constitutes an appendix to the Regulations of the sports event Ace Battle Run (TEAM MILE) TEAM-Individual Ranking (Poland) and the Public Provisions applicable to the “Team–Individual Ranking Mile (Team Mile Poland)” format. In matters not regulated by this Appendix, the general provisions of the Regulations and the Public Provisions shall apply, including those concerning the protection of personal data, the image of participants, safety and liability.

This document is of a general and informational nature and is intended for the internal use of the organizational team on the day of the Event. The document describes the sequence of technical and procedural activities of the personnel, the panel of judges and volunteers in preparing and conducting races in the “Team Mile Poland” format and does not replace the Event Regulations, the Public Provisions or other official documents addressed to participants.

CHRONOLOGY OF THE DAY OF THE START

Morning Race

Time Activity Status
8:30 Opening of the venue for personnel; commencement of operation of the competition office, timing system and medical point Technical readiness
9:00 Opening of registration for race participants and Check-in Beginning of admission
9:00–9:45 Verification of identity and completeness of the profile, issuance of the race number and chip, verification in the timing system, determination of roles in the race Admission to race formation
9:45 Closing of registration and Check-in New entries are not accepted
9:45–9:50 Verification of data concerning the first race, assembly of the team in the Call Room and issuance of MACE to Jokers Checking chips on the left leg and RUNNER roles by means of the wristband colour on the right hand
9:50–9:55 Departure of the team to the start Positioning according to roles
9:55–9:59 Assembly of Racers and Aces at the start line. Introduction of the team participants. Checking the positioning before the start, readiness signal from the REFEREES. The announcer announces the participant’s number, first name and surname, and role in the team; the TEAM RUNNER raises their right hand upon hearing their name. Positioning according to roles
9:59:00–9:59:30 The Starter positions everyone on the pre-start line Pre-start control by the judge at the start
9:59:30–9:59:50 Command “on your marks, attention”, gun signal. First start; subsequent starts every 15 minutes in accordance with the approved protocol The judge at the start gives the commands and fires the gun
After each race Collection of chips of the timing system. Publication of the preliminary result no later than 2 minutes after the actual finish Beginning of the 30-minute period for submitting a protest
After the last race End of the protest periods and publication of undisputed Official Results Results with the status Under Review are published after the decision
In the event of a delay All subsequent starts, protest deadlines and publications Unified actual time
Planned publication of all protocols on the website 30 min after the last race Publication of all Official Results Participants may leave the venue
