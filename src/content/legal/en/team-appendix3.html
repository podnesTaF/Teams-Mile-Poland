ACE BATTLE RUN (TEAM MILE POLAND)

This part constitutes an appendix to the Regulations of the sports event Ace Battle Run (TEAM MILE) TEAM-Individual Ranking (Poland) and to the Public Rules applicable to the “Team–Individual Ranking Mile (Team Mile Poland)” format. In matters not regulated by this Appendix, the general provisions of the Regulations and Public Rules shall apply, including those concerning the protection of personal data, the image of participants, safety and liability.

The team ranking competitions are conducted in three categories: men’s teams, women’s teams and mixed teams. Age subcategories do not apply. The prize pool for the qualification series amounts to PLN 10,000: PLN 8,000 for team categories (men’s teams – PLN 3,000, women’s teams – PLN 3,000, mixed teams – PLN 2,000), and PLN 2,000 for the individual category, men and women, for the three best ranking results.

The awarding of prizes shall take place on 10 October 2026, on the basis of the final classification established on the basis of the participant’s best official result during the ranking period.

1. CONDITIONS FOR PAYMENT OF THE PRIZE

The conditions for payment of the monetary prize include: confirmation of the team result status as “COMPLETED”; proper completion of the procedure for considering protests and appeals concerning the relevant result; and provision to the Organizer of the data necessary for payment and settlement of the prize, including the first name, surname, and bank account number of the Team Captain and — only where necessary for tax settlement purposes — the PESEL or NIP number.

The monetary prize shall be paid by bank transfer within 30 days from the date on which the final classification is established, unless the Organizer and the Team Captain agree on another payment date or method.

2. DISTRIBUTION OF THE PRIZE WITHIN THE TEAM

The monetary prize awarded to a team shall be divided among its participants by the Team Captain.

For the purposes of applying the income tax exemption provided for in Article 21(1)(68) of the Income Tax Act, the threshold of PLN 2,000 shall apply to the portion attributable to each individual participant of the team, and not to the total amount of the prize awarded to the team.

3. FAILURE TO CLAIM THE PRIZE

If a participant fails to provide the data necessary for payment of the prize within 90 days from the date of the prize-giving ceremony, the claim for payment of the prize shall expire, without prejudice to mandatory provisions of law.

The Organizer reserves the right to withhold payment of the prize for the duration of an investigation concerning a violation of these Rules by the relevant participant.

4. PUBLICATION OF RESULTS

In the public table of team classification results, the team name, as well as the first names and surnames of the participants comprising the team, the time, and the final team position in the ranking shall be published — on the basis and to the extent provided for in the GDPR Information Clause for Event Participants.

5. STATUS OF THE APPENDIX

  1. This Appendix No. 3 constitutes an integral part of the Public Regulations of the national ranking competition ACE BATTLE RUN – TEAM MILE POLAND.

  2. This Appendix shall apply in conjunction with § 14. “Categories and Prize Pool” of the main Regulations.

  3. In the event of any conflict between this Appendix and mandatory provisions of law, the provisions of law shall prevail.

  4. In the event of any conflict between this Appendix and the main Regulations with respect to a specific competition, the provisions of the main Regulations shall apply, unless the relevant ACE BATTLE RUN RATING rules expressly provide otherwise.

  5. The current version of this Appendix shall be published by the Organizer on the official information and registration resource poland.acebattle.run.
